Questions

Everything clients ask us first.

What does PhaseSave do?

PhaseSave is an alternative-energy consultancy in Palm Springs, California. We provide consulting, sales, engineering, and development for solar, thermal storage, zero-water cooling, and resilient microgrids.

Where is PhaseSave located?

PhaseSave is based at 195 S Civic Dr Suite, Palm Springs, CA 92262, serving the Coachella Valley, Southern California, and international projects across more than 15 countries.

What is PhaseStore thermal storage?

PhaseStore is modular thermal energy storage that charges 10°C graphite phase-change material (PCM) overnight at off-peak rates and discharges during peak demand. With SkyCool radiative panels it adds zero-water cooling, typically paying back in under two years.

How much can thermal storage save on cooling?

Savings come from shifting cooling load from on-peak to off-peak electricity. A 2,000 kW load running 6 peak hours at $0.34/kWh on-peak versus $0.08/kWh off-peak can save roughly $770,000 per year before incentives.

Does PhaseSave work on off-grid projects?

Yes. PhaseSave designs small off-grid self-install solar kits, battery-less off-grid systems for AC motors, solar wells, solar mining, and solar refrigeration.

Is the first consultation free?

About

PhaseSave is an alternative-energy consultancy based in Palm Springs, California, founded in 2004 — advising on solar, phase-change materials, thermal storage, zero-water cooling, and resilient microgrids.

We are a social entrepreneurship focused on bringing innovation to our communities and the planet — clean, efficient use of energy. We combine the experience of a senior solar engineer, an entrepreneurial physician, and honed business acumen to form a powerhouse solution provider.

We work in conjunction with our parent organization, Trinity Concepts Inc. — a Federal 501(c)(3) Community Development Financial Institution — to flex state, federal, and charitable programs toward community-beneficial renewable projects.
