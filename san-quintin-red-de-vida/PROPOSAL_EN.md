## The Opportunity in One Page

San Quintín is Mexico's youngest municipality (est. 2020), 32,883 km², ~117k residents + large migratory farmworker population, with **no municipal animal shelter**, a visible street-dog crisis, and a productive agricultural valley that pays to dispose of livestock mortality.

Instead of building a traditional “pound” (capture-kill-incinerate), San Quintín can leapfrog and set the **national standard** with a 5-layer mutual aid fabric:

1. **Solidarity Vouchers** — QR/paper vouchers for free spay/neuter at voluntary local vets (reimbursed in 72h)
2. **Neighborhood Pop-Up Clinics** — Mobile surgical units rotating through colonias/ejidos (40-60 surgeries/day)
3. **Wellbeing & Adoption Center** — No-kill intake: photo in <2h, 10-day reunion window, rehabilitation, adoption
4. **Aquamation** — Alkaline hydrolysis (water, not flame) for dignified remains; 90% less energy, zero emissions
5. **Mobile BioLiquidator + Fertilizer** — Trailer-mounted alkaline hydrolysis that converts mortality & unclaimed remains into sterile liquid fertilizer for parks and participating ranches (fertilization spreader)

**Closed loop:** Mortality → BioLiquidator (24h) → Sterile hydrolysate → Neutralized fertilizer → Parks/Fields → Ranches sponsor vouchers → More sterilizations → Less abandonment → Less mortality.

**Cost:** 5.2M MXN (~$275k USD) to launch; 4.2M MXN/year net to operate at scale (3,600 sterilizations/year). **Per capita: ~$1.60 USD/year.** Self-sustaining 40% by Year 2 via fertilizer value, private aquamation fees, and sponsorships.

## Why San Quintín, Why Now

- **Demand is explicit.** Local rescue collective *Huellitas de Amor SQ* publicly petitioned June 2026: “We need a Wellbeing Center, not a pound — we currently rescue from a private house.” No municipal facility exists.
- **Base capacity exists.** Baja California's Zoonosis Program sterilized 21,085 dogs/cats in vulnerable areas in 2024, including San Quintín. Surgical capacity is proven; what's missing is the permanent network.
- **Agricultural valley = circular economy.** Berry, tomato, and cattle operations generate mortality that is today a cost and health risk. Hydrolysis converts it to 2,500L of sterile hydrolysate per ton — validated to boost lettuce fresh weight +18% and pepper yield when diluted and co-applied.
- **New municipality = clean slate.** No legacy pound to reform. Whatever San Quintín designs now becomes its permanent regulation — and a model for Mulegé, San Felipe, and beyond.

## The 5 Layers (Detail)

### Layer 1 — Voucher Network

- **Nominative QR/paper voucher**, 30-day validity, covers 100% of spay/neuter at any affiliated vet. Tutor pays zero; vet reimbursed via SPEI in 72h at flat rate (cat 850 MXN, dog <15kg 1,100 MXN, etc.).
- **Eligibility:** Simple self-declaration + community health worker (*promotora*) validation. Auto-qualifies if in high-deprivation colonia, farmworker, senior, single mother, disabled, or community/feral animal. No humiliating paperwork.
- **3 ways to get it:** WhatsApp bot (photo + colonia → QR in <5 min), paper at store/church/promotora house, or on-site at mobile clinic.
- **Fund:** Mixed municipal + sponsorship (ranches/packers/hotels sponsor 50 vouchers/month) + 30% of fertilizer value. 3,600 vouchers/year ≈ 3.9M MXN.

### Layer 2 — Pop-Up Clinics

- **Mobile surgical trailer** (2 tables) or certified tent clinic, rotating weekly: Vicente Guerrero → Lázaro Cárdenas → Camalú → Bahía de los Ángeles.
- Team: 1 surgeon, 1 anesthesia, 2 techs, 3 promotoras. **40-60 surgeries/day**, HQHVSN quality, TNR ear-notch for ferals.
- **Cost per animal via mobile: 560-650 MXN** (cheaper than clinic voucher due to scale).
- **Territorial work:** 14 days prior outreach (door-to-door, posters, loudspeaker, WhatsApp group). Prioritizes females and street packs.

### Layer 3 — Wellbeing & Adoption Center (CEBISQ)

- **Not a pound. A Life House.** 48 dog kennels + 24 cat condos + quarantine, nursery, recovery, adoption living room, and memorial/aquamation suite. 800m² building + 2,000m² yards.
- **Flow:** Intake → Photo/video in <2h → Auto-post to FB/WhatsApp/board → Health/temperament eval 24-48h → Sterilize → 10-day reunion window (daily reposting) → If unclaimed, adoption pathway (interview + home visit + contract + 90-day follow-up). **No kill for space/time/breed.** Euthanasia only for irremediable suffering, with 2-vet + behaviorist sign-off and public record.
- **Capacity valve:** 20-30 registered foster homes + transfers to Ensenada/Tijuana/US partners when >85% full.
- **Target:** >30% reunion, >60% adoption of unclaimed, <22 days average stay.

### Layer 4 — Aquamation (Water Cremation)

- **Alkaline hydrolysis:** 95% water + 5% alkali, 93-98°C, 12-20h, gentle circulation. Bone mineral (ashes) returned; sterile effluent remains. Same natural process as burial, accelerated.
- **Vs flame cremation:** 90% less energy, zero dioxins/mercury/CO, 20% more ashes, silent, no smoke — compatible with urban area.
- **Two tracks at CEBISQ:** *Communal* (for unclaimed/community animals, ashes to collective urn/memorial garden, at municipal cost) and *Private* (tutor receives biodegradable urn + paw print, 800-2,200 MXN by weight vs 2,500-4,500 for out-of-town cremation). Subsidized aquamation vouchers for low-income.
- **Equipment:** Bio-Response PET-400 (180kg, 2 baskets, 2 cycles/day → 6-12 animals/day). 1.1M MXN refurbished. Small farewell room for ritual + monthly grief workshop.

### Layer 5 — Mobile BioLiquidator & Fertilizer Loop

- **Trailer-mounted 2,400L hydrolysis reactor** (20' trailer, diesel-heated, 90-100°C, atmospheric pressure). Processes 400-600kg/day (1 cow or 8 large dogs) in <24h. No pre-grinding, no transport of rotting carcasses — **bring the plant to the mortality.**
- **Output:** Sterile hydrolysate (pH >12.8 for 10+ days, destroys all relevant pathogens) + bone minerals. After neutralization with acidic silage (pH 3.9) or citric acid to pH 6.8 + 15-day aerobic treatment → amber liquid fertilizer (N 0.18-0.28%, amino acids 0.8%).
- **Validated:** Peer-reviewed trials show diluted hydrolysate + commercial fertilizer → +18.2% lettuce fresh weight, +chlorophyll/sugar, -50% fertilizer cost; improved pepper length/weight.
- **Three fertilizer lots:** Lot A for municipal parks (2-3L concentrate per 40L water per 1,000m²), Lot B for ranches (mixed with compost+biochar, applied via 3,000L slurry spreader), Lot C bone mineral amendment.
- **Yield:** 1 ton mortality → ~2,500L hydrolysate → 40,000L diluted fertilizer → 2-3 ha of park or 1 ha crop per cycle.
- **Economics Year 2 (80 tons/year):** 160,000L concentrate → 3.2M L diluted → ~872k MXN total value (fertilizer + avoided disposal). Covers 30-40% of Layers 1-3 operating cost.

## Governance & Finance

**Legal vehicle:** Nonprofit AC “Red de Vida San Quintín” in 3-year Collaboration Agreement with the Municipality.

- **Board (7):** 2 municipal (Health + Rural Dev), 2 rescue collectives, 1 vets college, 1 ejido/ranch, 1 academia (UABC)
- **Quarterly Community Assembly** (open, with live dashboard)
- **Common Fund:** Joint-signature account (AC + municipality), 3 earmarked subaccounts, SPEI-only, semiannual external audit, monthly public report
- **Diversified income (Year 2):** Municipal 45% + Sponsorships 20% + Private aquamation 12% + Fertilizer value 18% + Donations 5%

**Launch 5.2M MXN:** Facility 1.2M + Mobile clinic 0.55M + Aquamation 1.1M + BioLiquidator 1.35M + Initial voucher fund 1.0M

**Annual net at scale: 4.2M MXN (~$220k USD)** — less than operating a pound + incinerator + sporadic campaigns. Per capita **$1.60/year**.

**Municipal regulation to pass:** New *Reglamento de Protección y Bienestar Animal* that defines the Wellbeing Center, prohibits kill-for-space, establishes vouchers and mobile clinics as permanent programs, authorizes alkaline hydrolysis and fertilizer reuse, and legalizes TNR.

## Technology — Paper First

**Principle:** If it doesn't work with notebook + WhatsApp in a colonia with no signal, it doesn't work.

- **Paper:** Numbered voucher books with QR + carbon copy. Wall posters, handwritten intake log.
- **WhatsApp:** Single Business number, manual promoter replies at first (template), later WATI/Twilio bot. Photo + colonia → QR in hours.
- **Dashboard:** Static `site/index.html` on GitHub Pages, fed by CSV exported from Google Sheets. Public KPIs: vouchers issued/redeemed, surgeries by colonia, animals in shelter (photos), liters of fertilizer produced/applied, account statement. Updated daily, 10 min.
- **No app required.** PWA only after 12 months, when 300 vouchers/month flow without chaos.

## Implementation Roadmap

**Months 0-2 — Prep:** 1-day co-design workshop, form AC, sign agreement, 400-household census, recruit 8 promotoras, affiliate 3 vets.

**Months 3-5 — 90-Day Pilot:** 300 vouchers, 12 mobile clinic days, provisional 12-kennel center, 1 demo aquamation + 1 rented BioLiquidator week/month, live dashboard. **Target: 450 sterilizations, 40 reunions/adoptions, 2,000L fertilizer demo on 1 park.**

**Months 6-12 — Scale:** Full 48-kennel center + aquamation suite, owned BioLiquidator, 2 mobile days/week, 1,800 vouchers/semester, UABC fertilizer certification, 5 ranch fertilizer-for-voucher agreements.

**Months 12-24 — Consolidate:** 3,600 sterilizations/year sustained (45% valley coverage), >60% adoption, <5% euthanasia (medical only), 60-80 tons/year hydrolyzed → 150k L fertilizer, regulation passed, external evaluation + replication manual.

## Why This Matters

San Quintín doesn't need a bigger pound. It needs a fabric.

A fabric where the woman feeding 6 street dogs becomes a paid *promotora*, not a violator. Where the local vet earns from those who could never pay. Where a child who loses a dog receives ashes with dignity, not a black bag. Where a fallen cow doesn't pollute the arroyo but feeds next year's crop.

That's not “animalism.” It's **public health, circular economy, and municipal pride.** And it's replicable: if dispersed, young, low-budget San Quintín can do it, any municipality in Mexico can.
